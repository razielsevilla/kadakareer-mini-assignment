# KadaKareer Branding Guide

## 1. Brand Identity & Mission
* **Mission**: A low-bandwidth, community-based career development platform for underprivileged Filipino students.
* **Tone & Voice**: Encouraging, community-focused, approachable, and bilingual (English and Tagalog). 
  * Examples: *"Launch your career, kasama ang buong Kada"*, *"Maghanda para sa digital career path mo, for free."*

## 2. Color Palette
The KadaKareer color scheme is vibrant, welcoming, and professional, reflecting its youth-oriented and career-focused mission.

### Primary Colors
* **Deep Teal / Navy Blue**: `#0E4D78` / `#00497A`
  * *Usage*: Main brand color, used for headers, primary styling, and main backgrounds.
* **Neutral White**: `#FFFFFF`
  * *Usage*: Clean backgrounds for content sections and readability.

### Accent & Action Colors
* **Vibrant Orange**: `#F3AC5B` / `#F1A757` / `#F1D7B0`
  * *Usage*: Key accent color utilized for primary Call-to-Action buttons (e.g., "Sign Up"), text highlights, and graphical accents to draw attention.

### Supporting & Neutral Colors
* **Light Blue**: `#8BCBEB` / `#8EC8EF`
  * *Usage*: Illustrations, accent panels, and secondary highlights.
* **Dark Charcoal / Black**: `#201F1F` / `#010000`
  * *Usage*: Primary body text and dark UI elements to maintain high contrast and readability.
* **Muted Brownish Gray**: `#685D5D`
  * *Usage*: Minor text, subdued labels, or muted components.

## 3. Typography
The branding utilizes a mix of clean, modern sans-serif typefaces across its ecosystem to ensure accessibility, readability, and a friendly aesthetic.

### Web Application (`app.kadakareer.com`)
* **Stinger Fit** (Bold & Regular) - Distinctive custom brand font used for strong headings.
* **Manrope** (Variable Weight) - Highly legible primary interface and body font.

### Main Website (`www.kadakareer.com`)
The marketing website loads several Google Fonts to handle varied typographic hierarchies:
* **Montserrat**
* **Open Sans**
* **Lato**
* **Manrope**
* **Nunito**

## 4. Visual Elements & UI
* **Imagery & Illustrations**: Employs flat, approachable illustrations depicting community, learning, and career building (e.g., "career puzzles", "Kada Community").
* **Logos**: Features clean, modern SVG/PNG assets. A white logo variation is used consistently on dark/navy backgrounds, while the standard colored logo sits on light backgrounds.
* **Layout**: Utilizes clean, structured sections separated by bold color blocks (white, navy, light blue), ensuring an accessible, easy-to-navigate user experience.
